I previously wrote about 3D printing a Snoopy Omega Speedmaster-inspired clock: [3D Printing a Snoopy Omega Speedmaster]({% post_url 2026-06-08-omega-snoopy-clock %})

This time, I printed something a same-same, but different: a Rolex GMT-Master II wall clock.

Unlike some of my other projects, this was not an attempt to solve a problem, write software, or improve an existing design.

I just thought it was a cool design: [Rolex GMT-Master II – Wall Clock](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1005310-rolex-gmt-master-ii-wall-clock?from=search#profileId-1014593)

There was not much engineering or design work involved on my side for this one. The model was already available to download, so the project was simply a
matter of downloading the files and printing the pieces.
