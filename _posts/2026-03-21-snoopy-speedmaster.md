I built a custom chronograph around a Seiko VK63 movement.

The design was inspired by the Omega Speedmaster “Silver Snoopy Award” 50th Anniversary: a silver or white dial, blue chronograph details, a blue tachymeter-style bezel, and Snoopy as the centrepiece.

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/PXL_20260321_065504338.MP.jpg){:.centered}

## The Snoopy Speedmaster

Omega’s Speedmaster “Silver Snoopy Award” 50th Anniversary watch commemorates the Silver Snoopy Award that NASA astronauts gave Omega in 1970. The watch uses a silver dial, blue accents, blue subdials, and a Snoopy medallion at 9o’clock.

## The VK63 Movement

I built the watch around a Seiko Instruments VK63 movement.

The VK63 is a battery-powered meca-quartz chronograph movement. Quartz timekeeping provides accuracy and convenience, while the chronograph uses a mechanical module that gives the pushers and reset action a more mechanical feel than a conventional quartz stopwatch.

Its layout is useful for a Speedmaster-style design:

- **9 o’clock:** 60-minute chronograph counter
- **6 o’clock:** Running seconds
- **3 o’clock:** 24-hour indicator
- **Centre hand:** Chronograph seconds
- **4:30:** Date display

The VK63 does not duplicate the movement inside the actual Omega. The Omega uses a manually wound Co-Axial Master Chronometer Calibre 3861, while the VK63 is an accessible quartz-powered movement.

## Assembling the Watch

The project was an assembly build rather than traditional watchmaking from raw components.

I selected a case designed for the VK63, then paired it with a compatible dial, hands, crystal, bezel, crown, pushers, and strap or bracelet. The main work was making sure every part fit the movement and that the hands cleared one another properly.

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/PXL_20260321_033359197.MP.jpg){:.centered}

Hand installation required the most patience. Each hand needed to sit at the
correct height, avoid rubbing the dial or another hand, and align correctly
when the chronograph reset.
