After a fall trip to a chalet, I wanted to make a small physical reminder of it.

This is a model of a chalet designed in FreeCAD: a steep roof, a small chimney, a covered front area, windows, and a
railing around the deck. It is not intended to be an exact architectural model-just a compact version of the parts that made the original feel like a
chalet.

I printed the chalet with a 0.2 mm nozzle rather than the more typical 0.4 mm nozzle. The smaller nozzle made the small geometry more practical, especially around the railing, chimney, windows, and roof edges.
